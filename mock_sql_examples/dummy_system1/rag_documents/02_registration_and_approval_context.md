# Registration and Approval Lifecycle (Synthetic Demo)

## Registration is not approval
Registration is the intake of an operational request into a workflow. Approval is a distinct business decision on a previously recorded request. The existence of a registration record does not demonstrate that the request was approved or executed.

## Pending approval terminology
Pending approval denotes a request waiting for an approval decision. The term should not be confused with a pending documentary review. An implementation may use the same text label in more than one domain; the relevant table and workflow determine the interpretation.

## Scope and limitations
This document explains lifecycle vocabulary. It does not specify a registration guard, a threshold, a particular code, a mandatory review, or a required procedure call. Use SQL and authorized catalogs to determine such conditions.
