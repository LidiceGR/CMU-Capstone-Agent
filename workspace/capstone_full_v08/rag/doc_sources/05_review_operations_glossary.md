# Operational Review Vocabulary (Synthetic Demo)

## Review outcomes
A documentary review is an assessment of supporting material. Its possible outcomes are a domain separate from the lifecycle stage assigned to an operation. No numeric codes, thresholds, or transitions are prescribed in this glossary.

## Separation of concerns
An operational record may be described both by its registration stage and by the status of associated documentary review cases. Two distinct status fields must not be conflated merely because their values look similar.
